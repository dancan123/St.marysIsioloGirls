Office of the Bishop  |  Manager's Message

Pastoral Greetings from the Bishop's Office

I warmly extend my pastoral greetings to the administration, staff, students, parents, and all friends of this noble institution. As the Manager, I give thanks to God for the gift of education and for the dedicated service of the school administration who faithfully leads this school in the spirit of the Gospel.

The motto of our school, "Education is Empowerment," beautifully captures the Church's belief that education transforms lives. True education does not only impart academic knowledge but empowers young people to discover their God-given potential and to use their talents responsibly in service to humanity.

Guided by the vision of being a center of excellence in holistic education and mission, this school continues to form students intellectually, morally, spiritually, socially, and physically. Such holistic formation prepares learners not only for academic success but also for meaningful, fulfilling lives rooted in faith, discipline, compassion, and service.

The school mission — to create an enabling environment where learners acquire knowledge alongside spiritual, mental, physical, and social skills — reflects the heart of Catholic education. In a world often marked by uncertainty and moral challenges, this school stands as a place of hope, consolation, and values, where young minds and hearts are nurtured to become responsible leaders and witnesses of truth.

I commend the teaching and non-teaching staff for their selfless commitment, and I encourage students to take full advantage of the opportunities provided here. Let your education empower you to become agents of peace, justice, and positive change in the society.

As your Bishop, I assure you of my prayers and blessings. May God continue to console His people through this institution, and may the Blessed Virgin Mary guide and protect this school community always.

God bless you all.

"Console, Console My People" — Isaiah 40:1
Signed & Sealed
Peter Munguti Makau
