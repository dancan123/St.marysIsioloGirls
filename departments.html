School Administration

The administrative wing ensures the smooth day-to-day operation of the school, focusing on spiritual guidance, academic integrity, and student welfare under the Catholic Diocese of Isiolo.

Sr. Julia Mark
School Principal

Oversees the holistic growth of students, ensuring adherence to the school's mission of Faith and Excellence while managing institutional partnerships and spiritual life.

Mrs. Lucy Maina
Deputy Principal

The Finance department manages the school's resources with transparency and accountability, supporting infrastructural development and operational sustainability.

Mr. Simon Peter
School Bursar

Mr. Kelvin Otieno
